## My Centres
![Centres List](/C:/Users/Sanja/.gemini/antigravity/brain/1f21e26b-283d-402b-91d5-01a3a89ea32f/.system_generated/steps/1627/media_0.png)

The Centres screen displays all active field assignments. Tapping a centre routes to the Collection Sheet.

## Actions Available
- **Full Payment**: One-tap recording of exact dues.
- **Partial Payment**: Allows entering an ad-hoc amount.
- **Missed Payment**: Triggers the `CollectionAttempt` workflow, allowing officers to select reasons like "No Cash" or "Business Closed".
- **EOD Reconcile**: A dedicated page for declaring physical cash and bank totals before leaving the field.
